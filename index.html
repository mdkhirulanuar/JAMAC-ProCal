Formula Reference

Reference

Core formulas

  • CT Ratio = CT Primary ÷ CT Secondary
  • VT Ratio = VT Primary ÷ VT Secondary
  • Multiplier = CT Ratio × VT Ratio
  • Primary Constant = Nameplate Constant ÷ Multiplier
  • Energy = Pulse ÷ Constant × Multiplier
  • MD kW = kWh ÷ (Interval minutes ÷ 60)
  • Error % = (Meter Energy - Reference Energy) ÷ Reference Energy × 100

Field notes

Primary/billing reading: use no additional multiplier.
Raw meter pulse/nameplate constant: apply CT/VT multiplier.
SOP: Meter class tolerances and test acceptance must follow your organization/regulator procedure.
