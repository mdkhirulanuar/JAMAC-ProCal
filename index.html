Reference & Notes

Formula notes, assumptions and common mistakes.

Guide
Multiplier Formula

DIRECT: M = 1. CT: M = CT Primary / CT Secondary. CT+VT: M = CT Ratio × VT Ratio.

Pulse Constant

Primary-side equivalent constant = Meter nameplate constant / Total multiplier. Use this only when a primary-side pulse constant is required.

Energy Formula

Energy = Pulse / Meter Constant × Multiplier. Meter Constant must normally be the meter nameplate constant, e.g. imp/kWh or imp/kvarh.

Accuracy Test Assumption

Register Comparison compares MUT register difference against reference energy. Pulse Output Test converts MUT pulse into energy before comparison.

Common Mistakes
  • Do not double-apply multiplier.
  • Do not mix primary-side readings with secondary-side constants without conversion.
  • Verify CT secondary is usually 1A or 5A.
  • Verify VT secondary according to site design.
